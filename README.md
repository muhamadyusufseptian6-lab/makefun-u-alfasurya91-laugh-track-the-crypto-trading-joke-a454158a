One line, under 200 characters, about crypto and trading life — written for the
[Laugh Track: The Crypto Trading Joke Contest](https://make.fun/competitions/u-alfasurya91-laugh-track-the-crypto-trading-joke-a454158a)
on make.fun.

## The joke

> I bought the dip at $92k, averaged down at $88k, and by 3 a.m. my stop-loss had liquidated my position, my sleep schedule, and the last $12 of my dignity.

154 characters, one line. The setup is every trader's 3 a.m.: buy the dip,
average down, then watch the stop-loss take the position, the sleep schedule,
and finally the last $12 of dignity — in that order.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, deterministic) writes the single
joke line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out node main.ts
cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```
